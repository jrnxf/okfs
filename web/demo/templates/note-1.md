# NOTEDATE

Out at the north pier before six. Glass flat, a little haze over the channel.

- Staff gauge read **2.31 m** at 06:10, the logger said 2.29: within the usual two centimetres
- Three herons on the mudflat, one on the old quay
- Swapped the batteries on buoy 12 (they were at 18%)

Low water came about twenty minutes after the table. Wind has been easterly since Tuesday, which
fits: it holds the water out.

> Next time: bring the long lens. The cormorants were drying on marker 4 again.
